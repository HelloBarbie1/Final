TameableJungle

Welcome!

  • Tameable Jungle is a simple guide to how to make any living or working quarters into the luscious forest youve dreamed of. This site will include care basics, best plants for different spaces, and room setups for different lifestyles.
  • Choosing your space: Find plant ideas for homes, offices, apartments, and different lighting conditions.
  • Matching your lifestyle: Pick plants based on how much time and effort you can dedicate to their care. Too much care can be overwhelming. But little care can feel like a chore.
  • Building your indoor jungle: Use simple plant placement ideas to make your room feel grounded and alive.

Crazy/Unique Jungle Picks

  • Venus Flytrap: A carnivorous plant known for trapping insects.
  • Pink Princess Philodendron: Popular for its rare pink coloration.
  • Lithops: Tiny succulents often called “living stones.”
