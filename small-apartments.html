Small Apartment Plants

Space-Saving Tips

  • Use shelves and wall planters
  • Use hanging plants to save floor space while adding unique greens.
  • Choose smaller pots and slow growers
  • Tall and skinny plants fit perfectly in corners.

Best Plants for Small Spaces

  • ZZ Plant: This plant is glossy/shiny and handles low light and missed watering very well.
  • Pothos: A hanging plant that works perfectly on shelves or hanging planters.
  • Chinese Money Plant: Known for its round leaves and compact size, ideal for smaller rooms, who knows maybe itll grow some money too!
  • Lemon Button Fern: A small fern that works well on desks, shelves, and tabletops.
  • Succulents & Cacti: Excellent for sunny windows and low-maintenance lifestyles.
  • Air Plants: Requires no soil, truly a remarkable plant.
  • Nerve Plant: A colorful compact plant that grows well in humid spaces, either mist it frequently or place it in a bathroom.

My Top Five Apartment plants

  • Chinese Evergreen
  • Parlor Palm
  • Peperomia
  • Calathea
