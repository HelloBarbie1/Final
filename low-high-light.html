Low vs High Light Plants

How to Tell Your Light Level

  • Low light: few windows, far from windows
  • Medium light: bright room but not direct sun
  • High light: direct sun for hours near a window

High Light Plants

  • Succulents (with direct sun)
  • Cactus (bright window)
  • Bird of Paradise (if enough sun)
  • Fiddle Leaf Fig (if enough sun)

Low Light Plants

  • Snake Plant (very beginner friendly)
  • ZZ Plant (handles low sunlight well)
  • Pothos (unique falling vines)
  • Peace Lily (good for shaded rooms)
  • Chinese Evergreen (great for darker rooms)

Why Light Matters

  • Plants need different amounts of sunlight to stay healthy.
  • Too much direct sunlight can damage low light plants, killing them.
  • Matching plants to the correct environment improves growth and longevity.
