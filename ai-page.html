Help & support

How do I reset my password?

Go to the login page and click "Forgot password". You'll receive a reset link by email within a few minutes.

How do I upgrade my plan?

Navigate to Profile → Billing and choose the plan that works best for you. Changes take effect immediately.

Can I export my data?

Yes. Head to Settings and scroll to the Data section. You can request a full export at any time.
