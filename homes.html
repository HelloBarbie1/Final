Plants for Homes

Living Room Ideas

  • Place one tall plant in an empty corner to create a natural focal point.
  • Use medium-sized plants on shelves or tables to add greenery throughout the room.
  • Hanging/falling plants can soften spaces and make living rooms feel more relaxed and inviting.

Beginner Home Plants

  • Pothos are beginner friendly plants known to grow well in many home environments.
  • Snake Plants require a little bit of maintenance and they can tolerate lower light conditions at times.
  • Monsteras are popular for their large tropical leaves and assertive presence.
  • Peace Lilies as the name suggests, are great for creating a peaceful vibe in your home and they also help purify the air.

My Top Five Plants for Homes
