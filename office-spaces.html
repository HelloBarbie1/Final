Plants for Office Spaces

Setup Ideas

  • Place one medium plant near the corner of a desk for a cleaner workspace appearance.
  • Use trailing plants on shelves to add greenery without taking up desk space.
  • Choose pots with drainage trays to help avoid water spills and messes in office areas.

Benefits

  • Improves air quality by absorbing toxins in the office environment.
  • Reduces stress and increases productivity overall.
  • Enhances the overall aesthetic and vibe of the office space.

Light Conditions Check

  • Many offices have heavy overhead lighting, especially artificial blue light lighting, so low-light plants are often the best option.
  • Plants placed near windows or open sun no shade, usually grow healthier and maintain a more vibrant leaf color.
  • Rotating plants every couple weeks can insure that your plants receive balanced lighting exposure.
